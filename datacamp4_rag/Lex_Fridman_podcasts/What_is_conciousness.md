# What_is_conciousness.md

### Synopsis

The expert perspectives on consciousness reveal a rich and complex landscape of
thought, with varying interpretations and insights on its nature and function.
Many experts agree that consciousness is intricately linked to intelligence but
exhibit differing views on whether it is fundamental or emergent. Philosophers
like Philip Goff advocate for panpsychism, suggesting consciousness is a
fundamental feature of the universe present in all matter. In contrast, others
like Stephen Wolfram view consciousness as a form of bounded computation,
limited in time perception and awareness. Some experts emphasize the functional
aspects of consciousness—its role in intelligent behavior, moral consideration,
and adaptive purposes—while others, such as Karl Deisseroth and Donald Hoffman,
frame consciousness as an enigmatic phenomenon that poses both a scientific and
philosophical challenge. Overall, the discourse underscores the "hard problem"
of consciousness—how subjective experience arises from neural processes, and
whether our understanding of it can expand through future scientific inquiry.

### Expert Perspectives

**Nature of Consciousness**

*Panpsychism*

- "Consciousness is a fundamental and ubiquitous feature of physical reality, of
all matter in the universe." - **Philip Goff** (Episode #261: Consciousness,
Panpsychism, and the Philosophy of Mind)
- "If consciousness is just another player in the game of physics, it feels more
amenable to our scientific understanding of it." - **Philip Goff** (Episode
#261: Consciousness, Panpsychism, and the Philosophy of Mind)

*Emergent Property*

- "I don't think consciousness is just the ass end of a process... it has some
sort of important and highly adaptive function." - **Sheldon Solomon** (Episode
#117: Death and Meaning)
- "The general idea... is to figure out what complicated systems of matter...
could possibly lead to the emergence of consciousness." - **Donald Hoffman**
(Episode #293: Reality is an Illusion – How Evolution Hid the Truth)

*Computational Sophistication*

- "Consciousness is actually a step down from intelligence... we’re only
perceiving a sort of computationally bounded view of the universe." - **Stephen
Wolfram** (Episode #234: Complexity and the Fabric of Reality)
- "Consciousness is like the CEO that got an email at the end with the final
answer." - **Max Tegmark** (Episode #1: Life 3.0)

**Function of Consciousness**

*Adaptive Function*

- "Consciousness... has some sort of important and highly adaptive function." -
**Sheldon Solomon** (Episode #117: Death and Meaning)
- "It could be that consciousness provides a richer sense of self... creating
valuable adaptations." - **Karl Deisseroth** (Episode #274: Depression,
Schizophrenia, and Psychiatry)

*Hard Problem of Consciousness*

- "What is that inner state of subjectivity physically? What is it?" - **Karl
Deisseroth** (Episode #274: Depression, Schizophrenia, and Psychiatry)
- "This separation of conscious awareness from actual neural activity... poses
significant philosophical challenges." – **Jim Keller** (Episode #162: The
Future of Computing, AI, Life, and Consciousness)

*Illusion of Consciousness*

- "It's a post hoc narrative about what happened... it feels like it's behind
reality." - **Jim Keller** (Episode #162: The Future of Computing, AI, Life, and
Consciousness)
- "I tend to be sort of humbled by the mystery of it." - **Georges St-Pierre**
(Episode #179: The Science of Fighting)

This structured approach highlights the multilayered discussions surrounding
consciousness, presenting a well-rounded view of current expert thought on this
profound topic.
