# What are predicted timelines for AGI.md

### Synopsis

Predictions regarding the timeline for achieving Artificial General Intelligence
(AGI) vary significantly among experts, reflecting a spectrum of optimism and
skepticism. Some, like John Carmack, anticipate a 50-60% chance of seeing signs
of AGI by 2030, while others, such as Tomaso Poggio, are far more conservative,
suggesting a timeframe of approximately 200 years. Ben Goertzel highlights the
growing urgency of AGI discussions among national leaders and corporations,
indicating a cultural shift in the perception of AGI's importance. There is also
a notable concern about overhyping AGI's potential, as expressed by François
Chollet, who warns of possible investor disillusionment if expectations are not
met in the next 10 to 15 years. Overall, while there is excitement about the
possibilities of AGI, there remains a healthy skepticism about timelines and the
practical steps required to get there.

**Cautious Skepticism**
