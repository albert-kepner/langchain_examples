# What should an individual software developer learn to take advantage of developments in AI.md

### Synopsis

The landscape of opinion among experts regarding what individual software
developers should learn to capitalize on advancements in AI emphasizes several
key areas: foundational knowledge of AI technologies, an understanding of
machine learning applications, and proficiency in cloud computing. Experts like
Andrew Ng propose that developers should cultivate a general appreciation for
machine learning, suggesting that as AI permeates various industries, up to 50%
of developers may soon need to identify as AI developers in some capacity. This
sentiment is echoed by Kai-Fu Lee, who highlights the accessibility of AI tools
and the necessity of combining technical knowledge with business insight.
Conversely, Gary Marcus stresses the importance of understanding AI's
limitations for effective real-world application, urging a critical view of AI
capabilities. Additionally, Ben Goertzel proposes a shift toward agent-based
programming, indicating that developers should adapt to new paradigms in AI
software development. The consensus seems to be that both technical skills and
an appreciation of broader contexts (such as ethics and policy implications)
will become increasingly important for developers as AI evolves.

### Expert Perspectives

**AI Technical Knowledge**

*AI Basics*

- "I think the next wave of AI is for us to also transform all of those other
industries... so we need more teams to work with these." - **Andrew Ng**
(Episode #73: Deep Learning, Education, and Real-World AI)
- "The good news is that AI technologies are now more available in open source.
TensorFlow, PyTorch and such tools are much easier to use." - **Kai-Fu Lee**
(Episode #27: AI Superpowers – China and Silicon Valley)

*Understanding AI Limitations*

- "A lot of people in the deep learning community... have different levels of
misunderstanding of AI." - **Gary Marcus** (Episode #43: Toward a Hybrid of Deep
Learning and Symbolic AI)
- "I want machines to be able to ultimately communicate understanding with
humans." - **David Ferrucci** (Episode #44: IBM Watson, Jeopardy & Deep
Conversations with AI)

**Cloud Computing Proficiency**

*Cloud Appreciation*

- "I feel like the vast majority of software engineers today are sort of having
an appreciation of the cloud." - **Andrew Ng** (Episode #73: Deep Learning,
Education, and Real-World AI)

*Integration of Cloud and AI*

- "Smartphones... have definitely changed programming... I think we're still
dealing with a lot of the positive and negative ramifications of that." - **Jeff
Atwood** (Episode #7: Stack Overflow and Coding Horror)

**Adaptation to New Development Paradigms**

*Shift to Agent-Based Programming*

- "If you're willing to shift to an agent-based model of development, then you
can put less and less in..." - **Ben Goertzel** (Episode #103: Artificial
General Intelligence)

*Importance of Business Mindset*

- "You should have a more reasonable valuation expectation and growth
expectation... think less of this as a laboratory taken into a company." -
**Kai-Fu Lee** (Episode #27: AI Superpowers – China and Silicon Valley)

These expert perspectives collectively illustrate that software developers are
encouraged to expand their skill sets to not only include technical competencies
in AI and cloud services but also to adapt to evolving paradigms and understand
the broader implications of their work in AI technology.
