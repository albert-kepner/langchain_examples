# What will the impact of AI be on jobs.md

### Synopsis

The impact of AI on jobs is viewed through multiple lenses by experts,
highlighting both potential challenges and opportunities. Many foresee a
significant displacement of routine jobs—particularly in white-collar
sectors—while some express optimism about the creation of new opportunities in
service-oriented roles, especially in healthcare. There's a consensus that while
technological advancements will automate menial and dangerous tasks, the
necessity for societal shifts regarding training and compensation for these
changing job landscapes remains critical. Experts like Kai-Fu Lee argue for a
proactive approach to retraining, emphasizing the importance of preparing
workers for transitions into positions that are less likely to be automated.
Others, such as Steven Pinker, caution that the elimination of undesirable jobs
should not overshadow concerns about economic stability and workers' identity.

### Expert Perspectives

**Job Displacement vs. Job Creation**

*Displacement Concerns*

- "It's the routine jobs that will be displaced the soonest... when the workload
drops by that much, employment will come down." - **Kai-Fu Lee** (Episode 27: AI
Superpowers – China and Silicon Valley)
- "I do know that AI robotics will displace workers." - **Ayanna Howard**
(Episode 27: Human-Robot Interaction and Ethics of Safety-Critical Systems)

*Optimistic About Job Creation*

- "I hope that technology creates more opportunities than it destroys." - **Tim
Dillon** (Episode 155: Comedy, Power, Conspiracy Theories, and Freedom)
- "If we can eliminate horrible jobs... this is a fantastic boon to humanity." -
**Steven Pinker** (Episode 2: AI in the Age of Reason)

**Nature of New Jobs**

*Service-Oriented Roles*

- "There are going to be 2 million new jobs in healthcare services... and those
jobs are not likely to be filled." - **Kai-Fu Lee** (Episode 27: AI Superpowers
– China and Silicon Valley)
- "We've seen that the old economy is waking up and realizing that this is the
case." - **Juergen Schmidhuber** (Episode 11: Godel Machines, Meta-Learning, and
LSTMs)

*Challenges in Filling New Roles*

- "The social status of these jobs are not very good... they pay about half as
much as a heavy equipment operator." - **Kai-Fu Lee** (Episode 27: AI
Superpowers – China and Silicon Valley)
- "Work is a source of meaning... for a lot of people, losing their jobs means
losing their identity." - **Boris Sofman** (Episode 241: Waymo, Cozmo, Self-
Driving Cars, and the Future of Robotics)

**Societal Response and Adaptation**

*Retraining and Support Systems*

- "If we start thinking about retraining... it's going to be hard to deal with
the devastating losses that will emerge." - **Kai-Fu Lee** (Episode 27: AI
Superpowers – China and Silicon Valley)
- "One worry is... how do we get these people a decent income?" - **Steven
Pinker** (Episode 2: AI in the Age of Reason)

*Concerns About Long-term Economic Effects*

- "We’re going to have to create a platform that's healthy for that [social
discourse]." - **Tim Dillon** (Episode 155: Comedy, Power, Conspiracy Theories,
and Freedom)
- "The humans’ need for meaning and identity... is fundamentally about work." -
**Boris Sofman** (Episode 241: Waymo, Cozmo, Self-Driving Cars, and the Future
of Robotics)
